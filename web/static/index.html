Food safety evidence

Every number has a source.

ToxiTrace analyses food proteins and contaminants using published data only — structures from AlphaFold, molecular properties computed with RDKit, literature from PubMed, and limits from EU, FDA and FSSAI regulation. Nothing is estimated, and nothing is generated to look plausible.

Run an analysis What it does

What it is

A reader for evidence that already exists

Give it a food protein and a contaminant. ToxiTrace looks both up in public scientific databases, calculates what can be calculated exactly, finds the published research, and checks any measurement you supply against real regulatory limits — each one citing the law behind it.

Results arrive as a sourced report. Every figure carries the database, the accession and the retrieval time, so you can check it against the original.

What it won't do

  • No predicted binding affinities. Credible docking needs proper software and experimental validation. An invented number is worse than no number.
  • No overall risk score. A measurement compared against a published limit is checkable. A synthesised 0–10 score is not.
  • No filling of gaps. If a source has nothing, the report says so. Absence of evidence is a finding.

How it works

Six steps, in this order

The order is not presentational. A structure cannot be fetched before an accession is resolved, and nothing can be checked until the values exist.

  1. 01

    Resolve

    Your protein name becomes a reviewed UniProtKB entry with a stable accession. Your contaminant becomes a PubChem record with a real structure.

  2. 02

    Fetch

    The deposited AlphaFold structure is retrieved for that accession, with per-residue confidence — looked up, not folded locally.

  3. 03

    Compute

    RDKit derives molecular weight, logP, TPSA and Lipinski violations from the structure. Same input, same answer, every time.

  4. 04

    Cite

    PubMed is searched for work on that protein and contaminant. You get the papers that exist, with PMIDs — or an explicit note that none are indexed.

  5. 05

    Compare

    A measured concentration is checked against published maximum levels in the EU, US and India, each naming its legal instrument.

  6. 06

    Verify

    Every figure and record number in the written summary is checked back against what was actually retrieved. Anything that doesn't match is flagged, not printed quietly.
